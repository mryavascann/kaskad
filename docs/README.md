## How to read the archive

The submission and video READMEs describe the final delivered state. Storyboards retain the original recording instructions and planned scene times, clearly marked as archival; they are not remaining tasks or exact final-video transcripts. The `.txt` files contain only the original narration so they remain usable as source material.

The prepared panel text is preserved as a source archive, not asserted to be a byte-for-byte export of every final portal field. Final completion was separately verified through the actual Submit action and the portal state after reload.

Follow-on work such as a continuous keeper or production CRE deployment remains visible in the technical roadmap. **Finalized submission** describes delivery status; it does not turn those future capabilities into completed features.
