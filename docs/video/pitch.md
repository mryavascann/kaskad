## 00:00–00:15 · Takım ve problem

**Ekran:** Anlatıcı, iki takım üyesinin adı ve Kaskad adı.

> We are Marwa İpek and Rıfat Buğra Yavaşcan, the team behind Kaskad. We want lending teams to see what happens after a price shock, before a liquidation bottleneck becomes their problem.

## 00:15–00:36 · Neden bu problem

**Ekran:** Konsolda −%3 syrupUSDC; $111.0M stuck ve senaryo etiketi.

> A position can cross its liquidation threshold while liquidity is too thin to clear it profitably. In our recorded syrupUSDC scenario, a three-percent drop leaves about one hundred eleven million dollars stuck. A collateral ratio alone does not explain this modeled bottleneck.

## 00:36–00:56 · Çözüm

**Ekran:** Konsol sonuçlarından RiskOracle → GuardV2 → vault bölümüne geç.

> Kaskad simulates the cascade on Monad. Users can preview scenarios for free, verify recorded transactions, and see a risk oracle feed a circuit breaker and demo vault. The model and its limits are open.

## 00:56–01:14 · Neden Monad, ne geliştirdik

**Ekran:** Kısa gerçek benchmark kanıtı; ardından risk haritası/Perpl. Küçük etiket: “Blitz baseline → Metropolis additions”.

> We started at Monad Blitz Istanbul. Metropolis additions include the risk pipeline, CRE, historical replay, Perpl analytics, Nansen exposure and Mera accounts with encrypted watchlists. Our recorded Monad benchmark processes ten thousand positions in one transaction.

## 01:14–01:36 · İlk kullanıcı ve iş modeli önerisi

**Ekran:** Anlatıcı veya ürün ekranı; “First users: lending risk teams · vault builders” metni. Bu hedefler doğrulanmış müşteri değildir.

> Our intended first users are lending risk teams and vault builders. We plan to recruit design partners through reproducible case studies, compare alerts with existing monitoring, and test integrations in a sandbox. Paid monitoring could follow demonstrated usefulness; this is our proposed route to market.
